# Code of conduct

- Disagree about the code, the spec or a decision, not about a person.
- No harassment, personal attacks, discrimination or sexualised language in any project space.
- Do not post other people's private information, secrets, keys or real account credentials.
- Keep reports specific: what happened, what you expected, what you ran.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and block accounts that break these rules.
To report a conduct problem, open a private advisory at
<https://github.com/canhta/threadz/security/advisories/new> and say it is a conduct report.
This applies to the maintainer too.
